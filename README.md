# Lorem Ipsum Co. Stationary Company
## A dashboard created in Power BI illustrating a stationary company's fiscal overview and market analysis.

This repository houses a comprehensive Power BI dashboard ecosystem designed to take raw data from Lorem Ipsum Co. and turn it into actionable business intelligence. This dashboard delivers a multi-paged overview, illustrating the past year for the company. It delivers insight into revenue and expenses,and a full market analysis of countries Lorem Ipsum Co. provides to. The dashboard serves as a command center for strategic planners to seek profit maximization and other growth opportunities. This dashboard builds Lorem Ipsum Co. branding and visual storytelling, while enabling stakeholders and strategic planners to view this solution and it's KPIs seamlessly.

![addfile](https://github.com/hnsmith-cs/fiscal-example-dashboard/blob/main/loremipsum-preview.png?raw=true)

## Overview

Lorem Ipsum Co.'s dashboard translates raw information into strategic narratives. The questions that this dashboard will answer for the company are:

1. What seasonal patterns emerge when analyzing units sold per month?
2. Which product categories require immediate supply chain intervention based on COGs per Product?
3. Which segment provides the most revenue?
4. Have we met our total profit goal each quarter?

# Architecture

## Dashboard Construction

**Data Sources:** 'financials' Lab Import from Microsoft.

**Data Cleaning:** Anomaly and Error Detection, Power Query Transformation

**DAX Measures:** Dynamic Titles and Subtitles, Profit Goals for Each Quarter

**Visuals:** Power BI Lorem Ipsum Co.-specific Branding, Trend Charts and Matrix

## Technology Stack

**Power BI Desktop:** Data Modeling, Chart Rendering, Conditional Formatting, Data Source

**DAX Studio:** DAX Querying for Measures

# Key Features

## 1. Fiscal Overview - Home Page

- Units Sold Per Month
- Gross Profit and Gross Expenses per Product, Month, Country, and Segment
- Interactive filters to view each quarter and segment separately or together

## 2. Revenue and Expenses 

- Revenue per Month from Segments
- Total Revenue and Expenses per Month illustrated by a line and stacked column chart
- Revenue and Expenses per Product
- Discount Bands and Related Profits
- Target Profit and Total Profit

## 3. Market Analysis 

- Units sold per Product
- Total Revenue per Country and Segment
- Interactive Matrix of Product Revenue and Product COGs per country
